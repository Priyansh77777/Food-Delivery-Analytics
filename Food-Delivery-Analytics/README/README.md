# Food Delivery Operations Intelligence

## Business Problem

Food delivery companies frequently experience delays due to multiple operational factors such as traffic, weather conditions, restaurant preparation time, delivery distance, and courier availability.

The objective of this project is to identify the major drivers of delivery delays and recommend operational improvements that can help reduce delivery time and improve customer experience.

## Dataset Overview

This dataset contains food delivery order information including delivery distance, traffic conditions, weather conditions, vehicle type, preparation time, courier experience, and final delivery time.

## Project Goals

1. Identify the key factors affecting delivery time.
2. Measure the impact of operational variables on delivery performance.
3. Identify characteristics of delayed orders.
4. Recommend actions to reduce delivery delays.
